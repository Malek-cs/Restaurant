
🏛️ Lumiere Restaurant OS - Enterprise Edition
The Next-Generation Operating System for Modern Dining & Quick-Service Establishments.

Lumiere OS is a scalable, end-to-end restaurant management platform designed to streamline daily operations, maximize kitchen efficiency, and elevate the customer experience. Built with a modern technology stack, it provides real-time synchronization between the storefront, kitchen, and management dashboard.

🌟 Executive Overview
Frictionless Order Flow: Seamlessly route orders from the customer-facing web app directly to the kitchen display and delivery dispatch.

Zero-Commission Infrastructure: Maintain 100% of your revenue. Lumiere is a standalone OS, eliminating third-party aggregator fees.

Data-Driven Decisions: Integrated analytics dashboard to track daily revenue, popular items, and customer retention.

Omnichannel Communication: Built-in WhatsApp CRM for automated, zero-cost customer updates (Order Confirmed, Dispatched, Ready for Pickup).

🛡️ Core Modules & Capabilities
1. Advanced Order Management (OMS)
Real-Time Lifecycle Tracking: Monitor orders through 5 standard states: NEW ➔ CONFIRMED ➔ PREPARING ➔ READY ➔ OUT FOR DELIVERY ➔ COMPLETED.

Dynamic Load Balancing: Instantly pause incoming online orders during peak kitchen hours with a single click, without affecting your official business hours.

2. Menu & Inventory Control
Centralized Menu Editor: Instantly update items, categories, pricing, and high-resolution imagery.

Instant Stock Management: Mark items as Out of Stock to instantly reflect across all customer touchpoints, preventing canceled orders and customer dissatisfaction.

Smart Pricing & Taxation: Configure dynamic sales tax, service fees, and minimum order thresholds based on your region's compliance.

3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Secure your operational data with strict, role-based permissions:

Owner / Super Admin: Full access to financial analytics, system settings, and staff management.

General Manager: Access to daily operations, menu edits, and customer resolution.

Kitchen Staff: Restricted view focused solely on the Kitchen Display System (Incoming/Preparing orders).

Dispatch / Driver: Access limited to delivery routing, customer contact info, and status updates.

4. Marketing & CRM
Promotional Engine: Create and manage dynamic discount codes (Percentage or Fixed amount) to drive customer acquisition.

Customer Database: Automatically build a unified customer database with order history and contact details for future retargeting.

💻 Technology Stack & Security
Lumiere OS is engineered for high availability and security:

Architecture: Built on Next.js with Server-Side Rendering (SSR) for lightning-fast customer storefronts and SEO optimization.

Database: Powered by PostgreSQL & Prisma ORM, ensuring ACID compliance and robust relational data integrity.

Asset Storage: Secure local/cloud storage utility with automated MIME-type sniffing to prevent malicious file uploads.

Environment: Easily deployable via Vercel, AWS, or custom Linux VPS setups.
